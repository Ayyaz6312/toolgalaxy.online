How Does the ToolGalaxy PDF Merger Work?

Unlike many online PDF tools that require you to upload your files to a remote server, the ToolGalaxy PDF Merger operates entirely within your web browser. This means your documents never leave your device. The tool leverages advanced client-side JavaScript technology (specifically the pdf-lib library) to read, process, and combine your PDF files locally on your computer or mobile device. When you click the Merge button, the tool creates a new PDF document and sequentially copies all pages from each uploaded file into this new document while preserving the original formatting, layout, fonts, images, and vector graphics of every page. The result is a seamlessly merged PDF that looks exactly like your original documents placed together in order.

Tips for Best Results When Merging PDFs

To get the most out of the ToolGalaxy PDF Merger, consider these helpful tips. First, ensure all your PDF files are not password-protected or encrypted, as the tool needs read access to merge them properly. Second, check the file order carefully before clicking merge — use the drag-and-drop feature to arrange files exactly as you want them to appear in the final document. Third, if you are working with a large number of files, consider merging them in smaller batches for better browser performance, especially on older devices. Fourth, always download and verify the merged PDF to ensure all pages are present and correctly ordered before sharing or submitting it. Finally, keep your original files intact — the merging process creates a brand new PDF and does not modify your source documents in any way.
